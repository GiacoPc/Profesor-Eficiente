# Objective — KCA-ELS-INTEGRITY-HARDENING-001

## Primary objective

Design and validate, at contract level, an integrity control plane that prevents the specific failure class recently demonstrated: a governed AI audit workflow using a prompt, evidence package, context and repository state that do not provably correspond.

## Required outcome

Produce an independently auditable contract for:

- exact canonical-state identity;
- material-artifact identity and authority status;
- bounded bootstrap/context resolution;
- deterministic audit-package manifests;
- prompt/evidence compatibility;
- non-circular integrity manifests;
- freshness and stale-artifact detection;
- failure-closed semantics;
- governed continuation gating.

## Success condition

The contract is accepted only after direct evidence, independent L2 review, reconciliation and explicit human approval. Existence of documents is not operational proof.

## Current non-success states

`UNKNOWN`, `CONFLICT`, `FAIL`, missing evidence, stale identity, scope mismatch, or unavailable required evidence block convergence.
